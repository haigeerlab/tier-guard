## 3. Historical research and release records

[research/](research/) and [archive/](archive/) preserve dates, tested versions, original results and follow-ups. Old defaults, prices, models, paths and “unreleased” statements belong to their recorded context; do not use them as current installation instructions.

Suggested evidence entry points:

- [Historical compatibility snapshot](research/2026-09-12-v2-compatibility-status.md): September 2026 mechanism evidence; use compatibility for current status.
- [New-catalog host rechecks](research/2026-10-05-catalog-rehost-verification.md): October 2026 dispatch and pin evidence.
- [Codex L2 task experiment](research/2026-10-05-codex-l2-luna-experiment.md): evidence for current Luna/high L2 capability.
- [Dispatch economics verdict](research/2026-10-06-dispatch-verdict.md), [joint cost experiment](research/2026-10-06-spec-guard-joint-cost-test.md): economic outcomes for specific tasks, not a stable savings guarantee.

Research text and raw data retain their original language. The bilingual compatibility guide provides the conclusions and limitations needed by users.
